Rules

1
Tap = Instant Stop

If a fighter taps — hand, foot, or verbal — the match is over immediately.

2
The Referee Has Full Control
  • Can start and stop the fight
  • Can pause the action at any time
  • Calls submissions and stoppages
  • Separates fighters when needed
3
Round System

Every bout runs three two-minute rounds.

4
Illegal Actions
  • Slams (that aren't on couches)
  • Eye gouging
  • Any striking
  • Small joint manipulation (fingers/toes)
5
Stoppages — How A Fight Is Won Or Lost
  • TKO — if a fighter is not intelligently defending themselves
  • Submission
  • Decision — after 3 rounds, the judges decide
  • Disqualification — for performing illegal actions
